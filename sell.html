    Keep these papers ready

    Buyers and their banks will ask for these. Having them ready can save weeks.

    • Sale deed or registry copy

      And the previous chain of deeds, if the property has changed hands before.

    • MCD property tax receipts

      The latest receipt, ideally with the last few years paid.

    • Conversion status

      Whether the property is freehold or leasehold, with the conversion deed if converted.

    • Electricity and water bills

      Recent bills in the owner's name, with no dues pending.

    • Builder agreement, for new floors

      The collaboration agreement, completion or occupancy certificate if issued, and parking allotment.
