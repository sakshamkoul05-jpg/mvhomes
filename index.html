Buying through MV Homes

From first call to registry

Every purchase goes through the same six steps. You always know what comes next and what it costs.

STEP 1

Requirement

Budget, colonies, floor, parking and possession date. One conversation, written down.

STEP 2

Shortlist

Only properties that fit. Plot size, floor, facing and age shared before you travel.

STEP 3

Site visits

We go with you, on your schedule, and point out what to check: seepage, lift, stilt height.

STEP 4

Title check

Chain of sale deeds, MCD property tax and conversion status reviewed before any token.

STEP 5

Token & agreement

Negotiation, token receipt and Agreement to Sell with clear payment milestones.

STEP 6

Registry & keys

Stamp duty, appointment at the Sub-Registrar, mutation and handover of keys.
