    Buying through MV Homes

    Six steps down to the registry

    STEP 1

    Requirement

    Budget, colonies, floor, parking and possession date, written down in one conversation.

    STEP 2

    Shortlist

    Only homes that fit. Plot size, floor, facing and building age shared before you travel.

    STEP 3

    Site visits

    We go with you and point out what to check: seepage, lift make, stilt height, sunlight.

    STEP 4

    Title check

    Chain of sale deeds, MCD property tax and conversion status reviewed before any token.

    STEP 5

    Token & agreement

    Negotiation, token receipt and an Agreement to Sell with clear payment dates.

    STEP 6

    Registry & keys

    Stamp duty, Sub-Registrar appointment, mutation, and the keys in your hand.
